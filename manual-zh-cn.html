热水器遥控控制 / Water Heater Remote Control

  1. 按下"热水开/关"按钮，打开热水。
    Press the HOT WATER ON/OFF button to turn on the hot water.
  2. 将热水龙头转到热水一侧。
    Turn the hot water tap to the hot side.
  3. 稍等几秒钟，热水即会流出。
    After a few seconds, hot water will start running.
  4. 使用完毕后，再次按下"热水开/关"按钮关闭。
    When you finish, press the HOT WATER ON/OFF button again to turn it off.

按钮说明 / Button Guide

  • ・TEMP UP / TIMER UP：温度调高 / 定时器调高
  • ・TEMP DOWN / TIMER DOWN：温度调低 / 定时器调低
  • ・HOT WATER ON/OFF：热水开/关
  • ・TIMER ON/OFF：定时器开/关

燃气灶使用方法 / How to Use the Gas Stove

  1. 开启抽油烟机或打开窗户通风。
  2. 选择炉口，向ON方向按压并旋转旋钮点火。
  3. 保持旋转数秒直至火焰稳定，然后松开。
  4. 转动旋钮调节火力大小（HIGH/LOW）。
  5. 烹饪完毕后，将旋钮转回OFF并确认火焰完全熄灭。

Safety Notes / 安全注意

  • 烹饪时请勿离开灶台。
    Do not leave the stove unattended while cooking.
  • 请勿将纸张、塑料、油等易燃物放在火焰附近。
    Keep flammable items away from the flame.
  • 请勿让儿童单独使用燃气灶。
    Do not let children use the gas stove.

电热地毯使用方法 / How to Use the Electric Floor Heater (Hot Carpet)

  1. 打开电源/选择加热区域
    将控制器开关从"关(OFF)"调到您想要的加热区域（如：全面/单侧）。
    Move the area selector switch from OFF to your desired heating area (for example, full area or one side only).
  2. 设置温度
    用"温度调节"旋钮（弱・中・强）调节到您喜欢的温暖程度。
    Use the temperature control (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) to set your preferred warmth.
  3. 等待加热
    设置后稍等几分钟，地板会逐渐变暖。
    After a few minutes, the floor will gradually become warm.
  4. 关闭时
    使用完毕后，将开关拨回"关(OFF)"，长时间不用时请拔掉插头。
    After use, turn the switch back to OFF, and unplug the hot carpet if you will not use it for a long time.

※ 安全自动断电功能：此电热地毯在连续使用约8小时后会自动断电。如需继续使用，请再次打开开关。
For safety, this hot carpet has an automatic shut-off function after about 8 hours of continuous use. If needed, you can turn it on again with the switch.

电路断路器说明 / About the Circuit Breaker

同时使用多种电器时，断路器可能会跳闸断电。
If you use too many electrical appliances at the same time, the circuit breaker may trip and the power will go off.

如果发生断电，请先关闭当时正在使用的电器电源。
If this happens, first turn off the appliances you were using.

本设施使用智能电表，断路器跳闸后约10秒会自动恢复供电。
This room uses a smart meter, so the power will usually be restored automatically in about 10 seconds after the breaker trips.

如果约10秒后电力仍未恢复，可能是某个独立断路器跳闸了。
If the power does not come back after about 10 seconds, one of the individual breakers may have tripped.

请查看玄关上方的配电箱，将向下的开关拨回向上位置。
Please check the breaker panel above the entrance and push any switches that are in the DOWN position back to the UP position.

如果电力仍未恢复，请联系房东。
If the power still does not come back, please contact the host.

用语说明 / Terms

  • ・主幹漏電ブレーカー → 主断路器（漏电保护）/ main breaker (earth leakage protection)
  • ・個別ブレーカー → 独立断路器 / individual breaker

婴儿椅安装・拆卸方法 / How to Attach / Remove

安装方法 / How to Attach:

  1. 将桌面插入防滑盖（Non-slip cap）和固定旋钮（Locking knob）之间。
    Insert the tabletop between the non-slip cap and the locking knob.
  2. 将固定旋钮逆时针旋转固定。
    Turn the locking knob counterclockwise to secure it.

● 请务必将桌面插至防滑盖根部。
Be sure to insert the tabletop all the way to the base of the non-slip cap.

拆卸方法 / How to Remove:

  1. 将固定旋钮顺时针旋转（与安装方向相反）。
    Turn the locking knob clockwise, in the opposite direction used for attachment.
